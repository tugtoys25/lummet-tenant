Analytics

When on, visitors can accept or reject analytics. Until they accept, Google Analytics runs in its cookieless consent mode, and a "Cookie settings" link in the footer lets them change their mind. Leave off if you do not use analytics or already have another consent tool.

Enter your GA4 Measurement ID, optionally followed by a Google Tag / container ID separated by a comma (e.g. G-E0ZDHXWWX7, GT-PLFFPKPD). Leave blank to disable tracking.
