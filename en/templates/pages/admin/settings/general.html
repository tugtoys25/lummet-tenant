Stops automated visitors from being counted as real clicks on /en/go/... links. Real visitors are never affected. Off by default. When on, robots.txt also asks crawlers to stay away from tracked links. Flagged clicks stay visible in analytics and are left out of earnings.
Default is /en/ and nothing changes until you save a different choice. Dashboard and API addresses always stay on /en/. Old /en/ links keep working and redirect to the new address.
One rule per line: /page = root, /page = en or /page = custom. A rule covers that page and everything below it.
