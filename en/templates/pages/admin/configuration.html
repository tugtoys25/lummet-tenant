Setup progress

    How setup works
    1. Pick a type. A type (preset) switches a ready-made set of modules on or off. Your site was created with one, or with a copy of another site of yours.
    2. Fine-tune. Switch modules, page types and tools on or off. Changes are saved to your draft; visitors keep seeing the live site.
    3. Review and publish. Publishing makes the draft live as a numbered version. Nothing is deleted when you switch something off.
    4. Undo. Open the History tab and restore any earlier version. Restoring is itself a new version.
    5. Confirm. On a new site, press This is right, confirm in the welcome panel once you are happy. It only removes the welcome panel.

    Plan defaults and copying a site are chosen by your platform administrator when the site is created. If the whole configurable platform is switched off for this site, this screen is hidden and the site runs every module exactly as before; your saved choices are kept.
