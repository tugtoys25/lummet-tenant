Advanced Homepage

Build a modern, conversion-focused homepage from ready-made sections, then preview it exactly as visitors will see it. Your current homepage is never changed: this one only goes live when you press Publish to live site, and you can switch back in one click.

ⓘ How to use this page

This screen has the section editor on the left and a live preview on the right.

  1. Edit. Open a section, switch it on or off with its tick box, and use the arrows to change its order. Every field has a one-line hint, and each section has its own guide.
  2. Preview. The preview updates a moment after each change. Use Preview as country to see what a visitor from another country gets, and the Desktop, Tablet and Mobile buttons to check each size.
  3. Save. Saving stores your settings but does not change the live site.
  4. Publish. Publish to live site makes visitors see this homepage. Switch back to default homepage returns to the current one instantly. Undo last change goes back one step.
