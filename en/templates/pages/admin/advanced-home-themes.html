Advanced Homepage Themes

Ready-made looks for the Advanced Homepage: colours, section backgrounds, headings, bands and button shapes. A theme here only changes the homepage content area. Your header, footer and every other page keep their own look, and the themes in Premium Themes are not affected.

ⓘ How to use this page
  1. Browse. Use the All, Light and Dark buttons to narrow the list. Each card shows the theme's colours.
  2. Preview. Press Preview on a card to see your real Advanced Homepage in that theme on the right. Nothing is saved yet. Use Preview as country and the Desktop, Tablet and Mobile buttons to check each case.
  3. Apply. Press Use this theme, then Save theme. No theme returns to your site's own colours.
  4. Go live. Themes style the Advanced Homepage, so it must be published. Publish or switch back on the Advanced Homepage page.

Good to know: the theme settings are saved together with your Advanced Homepage settings, so Undo last change on that page also undoes a theme change. Card styles still come from Card Studio, and the hero from Hero Studio or Header & Hero.
